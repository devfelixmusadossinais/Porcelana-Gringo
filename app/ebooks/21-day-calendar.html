SAFETY

Your skincare ground rules

Simple precautions for a comfortable routine.

Sunscreen, every day

Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning, rain or shine. Reapply as directed. No homemade recipe replaces sunscreen.

Clean hands, clean tools

Wash your hands and use clean, dry bowls, spoons, and containers.

Make it fresh

Use homemade mixtures immediately and discard leftovers. For products you keep, choose commercially formulated creams and lip balms; follow their labels.

Sensitive or broken skin

Avoid broken or irritated skin and known allergens. If burning, itching, or redness occurs, rinse off and stop using the mixture. Consult a dermatologist if symptoms persist, or before starting if you have a skin condition.

Important: this planner is for cosmetic care and general information, not medical advice. Results vary, and no particular change in appearance or feel is guaranteed. It does not replace a dermatologist’s guidance. Always do a 24-hour patch test before using a new ingredient or mixture. A patch test cannot rule out every reaction.

An easy place to start

Featured recipe
Oat Cloud Mask
TIME

Up to 15 minutes

HOW OFTEN

1 to 2 times per week, if comfortable

USE BY

Use immediately; discard leftovers

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp plain quick oats
  • 2 tbsp pasteurized milk or plain, unsweetened plant-based milk
Method
  1. 1Grind the oats into a fine powder.
  2. 2Add the milk a little at a time until you have a creamy paste.
  3. 3Let sit for 3 minutes to thicken.

How to use: With clean hands, spread a thin, even layer over clean skin. Avoid eyes and lips. Rinse gently with lukewarm water; do not scrub.

Caution: Skip this recipe if you are allergic to oats, dairy, or any ingredient in your chosen milk. Do a 24-hour patch test before first use: apply a small amount to your inner arm, rinse after the intended wear time, and watch the area for 24 hours. This does not rule out delayed reactions; AAD guidance recommends testing new skincare products for 7 to 10 days. Rinse off and stop using if irritation occurs. Ask a dermatologist before trying homemade skincare if you have a skin condition, and seek advice for persistent irritation.

Fresh Glow Aloe Gel
TIME

10 to 15 minutes, following the product label

HOW OFTEN

3 to 4 times per week, if comfortable and allowed by the label

USE BY

Use dispensed portions immediately

Ingredients
  • Up to 2 tbsp packaged, fragrance-free, alcohol-free aloe vera gel
  • Up to 1 tsp Korean Glow Water: store-bought hydrating toner, applied separately
Method
  1. 1Wash your hands. Choose aloe gel labeled for facial use. Follow the package directions.

How to use: Apply a small amount of toner to clean face and neck; let dry. Follow with a thin layer of aloe gel, avoiding eyes and lips. For a rinse-off step, leave on for 10 to 15 minutes only if the label allows, then rinse with cool water. Leave on only if directed by the label.

Caution: Do not use gel scooped from a fresh aloe leaf or any yellow sap. Do a 24-hour patch test before first use: apply each product to a separate small spot on your inner arm, follow its wear and rinse directions, and watch for 24 hours. This does not rule out delayed reactions; AAD guidance recommends testing new skincare products for 7 to 10 days. Stop using if burning, itching, or redness develops. Featured recipe
Silky Lip Balm
TIMING

Leave on once fully cooled

HOW OFTEN

Whenever your lips need it

STORAGE

For same-day use, in a clean, dry container

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 tsp grated beeswax (or a cosmetic-grade plant wax suitable for lip balm)
  • 1 tsp sweet almond oil
Method
  1. 1Gently melt the coconut oil and wax in a heatproof bowl over barely simmering water, keeping water out.
  2. 2Take off the heat, add the almond oil, and stir.
  3. 3Pour into a clean, dry container. Let set for at least 20 minutes, until completely cool and firm.

How to use: Apply a thin layer throughout the day and a slightly thicker layer before bed. If your lips feel flaky, briefly hold a soft cloth dampened with lukewarm water against them, then pat dry. Skip scrubbing.

Caution: Patch test for 24 hours before use. Avoid any ingredient you are allergic to. Scoop out with a clean, dry spatula and keep water out of the container. Discard leftovers at the end of the day. Stop using if irritation occurs; consult a dermatologist if dryness, cracking, or irritation persists.
