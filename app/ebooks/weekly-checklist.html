KOREAN SILK METHOD · HYDRATE · NOURISH · REJUVENATE · ONE WEEK AT A TIME
How it works

A steady routine starts with a little rhythm.

Getting started is one thing. Keeping track of 30 recipes is another. This checklist gives you one page per week, organized around the method's 3 layers. As your skin's preferences shift in your 40s, 50s, and beyond, you can choose what feels comfortable instead of doing everything. Pick your recipes, check off each use, and stay within the menu's limits. On Sunday, take a moment to notice how your skin feels and adjust the week ahead. Consistency matters more than perfection, and skipping an optional recipe is always okay.

Your weekly rhythm

Four simple steps

Choose what feels comfortable. Skip anything else.

1
Start with the basics

Check off gentle cleansing and your morning broad-spectrum SPF 30+. Use a moisturizer that feels comfortable, and reapply sunscreen as directed.

2
Choose your optional extras

Pick a facial water, gel, or mask you've already patch-tested. Write its name on the blank line and check off the days you use it. You don't need something from every layer.

3
Stay within the limit

Each recipe has a frequency limit in the Recipe Menu. It's a ceiling, not a goal. Once you reach it, skip that recipe for the rest of the week; you don't need to replace it.

4
Sunday: a 5-minute check-in

Note how your skin felt, check your supplies, and adjust next week. More products don't always mean more comfort.

Your skin type
Dry skin

You may prefer the richer textures in Layer 2. Choose what leaves your skin feeling comfortable.

Oily skin

Start with the lighter options in Layer 1, and use only as much as feels comfortable.

Combination skin

Adjust by area: lighter textures where skin feels oily and a little more moisturizer where it feels dry.

Busy week?

Gentle cleansing, a familiar moisturizer, and broad-spectrum SPF 30+ in the morning. Check it off and keep going. Homemade extras are optional.

The 6 golden rules
1
Patch-test first

Before first use, apply a small amount to your inner forearm and monitor for at least 24 hours. Rinse off at the recipe's stated time; don't leave a rinse-off mask on for 24 hours. Don't use it if you notice itching, burning, redness, or swelling. A patch test can't rule out every reaction.

2
Sunscreen, every day

Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning, rain or shine. Reapply according to the label, at least every 2 hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating. No homemade recipe replaces sunscreen.

3
Keep everything clean

Wash your hands and use clean, dry bowls, spoons, and containers. Don't share mixtures, double-dip, or add water to stored commercial products.

4
Make it fresh

Make homemade mixtures for one use and discard leftovers. A refrigerator doesn't make an unpreserved mixture safe to store. Never rely on smell alone to judge safety.

5
Check with your dermatologist

If you have sensitive skin, a skin condition, or are pregnant, ask a dermatologist before trying these recipes. Avoid broken or irritated skin and known allergens. Keep food-based mixtures away from eyes, eyelids, and lash lines.

6
Keep expectations realistic

Any benefits refer only to how skin looks or feels, such as temporary softness or a smoother-looking finish. Results vary. This is cosmetic care, not medical advice.

Your Sunday check-in

Listen to your skin

Your Sunday check-in, in three colors.

How does my skin feel?
Green · keep it comfortable

Your skin feels comfortable and soft, without tightness.

Keep your routine steady. If you'd like to try something new, patch-test first and introduce just one recipe at a time.

Yellow · take a break

Your skin feels tight, dry, or weighed down.

Pause the homemade extras and return to gentle cleansing, a familiar moisturizer, and daytime SPF 30+. If rice water leaves your skin tight, stop using it. Wait until your skin feels comfortable before considering another recipe.

Red · stop

You notice burning, itching, redness, or swelling.

Rinse gently with cool water and stop using the mixture. Don't apply homemade products to irritated skin. Contact a dermatologist if symptoms persist or worsen. Seek urgent medical help for trouble breathing or swelling of the lips, tongue, or throat.

Before trying a new recipe
1
One at a time

Introduce one new recipe at a time so it's easier to notice what feels comfortable and what doesn't.

2
Patch-test first

Test a small amount on your inner forearm and monitor for at least 24 hours before facial use. Follow the stated rinse-off time. Discard the test batch and prepare a fresh amount for your face.

3
Give it 1 week

Wait at least one week before adding another new recipe, and only continue if your skin stays comfortable. Stop sooner if you notice irritation.

When life gets busy, keep it simple: gentle cleansing, a familiar moisturizer, and broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning. Check it off and move on with your day. Optional recipes can wait. Any cosmetic benefits are about appearance and feel, and results vary.
