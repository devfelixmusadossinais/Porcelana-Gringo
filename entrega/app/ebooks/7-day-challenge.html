Welcome

Seven days. A little time for you.

With 30 recipes in the ebook, figuring out where to start can feel like the hardest part. This Challenge gives you a seven-day guide, with a clear order to follow. Each day includes your plan for morning and evening, what to get ready ahead of time, and today's recipes with ingredients and directions. Check off what you do, jot down how your skin feels, and reflect at the end. Whether you're in your 40s, your 50s, or beyond, let your skin's comfort set the pace. Introduce one new mixture at a time, after testing, and stretch the schedule out as needed. Homemade skincare is optional cosmetic care, and results vary. On busy days, stick with gentle cleansing, your usual moisturizer, and sunscreen. Consistency matters more than perfection.

Before you start

How the Challenge Works

Four simple guidelines. One step at a time.

1
Two moments each day

A short morning routine, the Morning Refresh Ritual, and an optional evening mask. Skip any step your skin doesn't welcome.

2
Get ready the night before

Check tomorrow's ingredients and clean your tools. Make fresh, single-use portions when needed; these homemade mixtures don't have verified storage periods.

3
Patch test first

Before Day 1, test new ingredients and each finished mixture on a small area and monitor for at least 24 hours. This is the ebook's first safety rule. A 24-hour check cannot rule out delayed reactions; allow longer testing before starting.

4
Sunscreen, every day

Finish every morning with broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen, rain or shine. Reapply according to the label, at least every 2 hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating.

Get Ready

Gather your essentials before Day 1. Start testing early enough to allow extra time.

Patch Test · At Least 24 Hours

Test a small amount of each new ingredient and each finished mixture on separate spots on your inner forearm. Follow the intended contact time and rinse off rinse-off mixtures; don't leave a mask on for 24 hours. Monitor for at least 24 hours, and skip anything that causes itching, burning, redness, or swelling. An initial 24-hour check doesn't rule out delayed reactions. Allow longer testing before facial use, following a dermatologist's guidance. Make a fresh portion for each test. Don't test known allergens. If you have sensitive skin, a skin condition, or are pregnant, ask a dermatologist before starting.

  • Plain honey

Refresh & Hydrate

Recipe of the day
Aloe Comfort Gel
Time10 to 15 minutes
How often3 to 4 times a week, as tolerated
Shelf lifeUse immediately and discard leftovers
Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp 100% pure aloe vera gel with no added fragrance, color, or drying alcohol
  • 1 tsp freshly made Rice Water Glow or distilled water
Method
  1. 1Use commercially prepared aloe gel rather than gel scooped from a fresh leaf; leaf sap and home preparation can introduce irritants or contamination.
  2. 2Stir the gel in a clean bowl with a clean fork until smooth.
  3. 3Mix in the fresh rice water or distilled water until the gel loosens slightly.

How to use: Spread a thin layer over clean face and neck skin, avoiding your eyes and lips. Leave on for 10 to 15 minutes, rinse with cool water, and follow with moisturizer.

Heads up: Patch-test at least 24 hours before first use. Diluted aloe gel isn't protected by the original product's preservative system. Don't store this mixture or use it on sunburned, broken, or irritated skin.
Recipe of the day
Eye-Area Comfort Dab
Time1 minute
How oftenMorning and evening, subject to the eye moisturizer's label directions
Shelf lifeUse dispensed portions immediately and discard excess; follow each original product's expiration instructions
Ingredients
  • 1 tsp fragrance-free moisturizer specifically labeled for the eye area, kept in its original container; dispense only the amount directed on its label
  • 2 drops pure jojoba oil for the upper cheeks only
Method
  1. 1Keep the eye moisturizer and jojoba oil separate. Don't blend them or add oil to the moisturizer's container.
  2. 2With clean hands, dispense only the label-directed amount of eye moisturizer and the oil you'll use. Don't return any dispensed leftovers to their containers.

How to use: Apply the eye moisturizer exactly as its label directs, using a light touch and staying clear of your eyes and lash lines. Separately, press the jojoba oil onto dry upper cheeks below and outside the bony eye socket. Keep the oil away from your eyelids and eyes.

Heads up: Check tolerance on a small skin area at least 24 hours before first use, following product directions. Homemade aloe-and-oil blends aren't formulated for eye use. If burning, redness, or watering occurs, rinse with water and stop. Get medical care for eye pain or vision changes.
Recipe of the day
Oat Cloud Mask
Time15 minutes
How often1 to 2 times a week, as tolerated
Shelf lifeUse immediately and discard leftovers
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp plain rolled oats, finely ground
  • 2 tbsp pasteurized milk or plain, unsweetened oat milk
Method
  1. 1Grind the oats into a fine powder, or use plain oat flour. There shouldn't be any scratchy pieces.
  2. 2Stir in the milk gradually until you have a soft cream.
  3. 3Let stand for 3 minutes to thicken.

How to use: Spread an even layer over clean skin, avoiding your eyes and lips. Rinse gently with lukewarm water without scrubbing, then follow with moisturizer.

Heads up: Patch-test at least 24 hours before first use. Skip this recipe if you're allergic to oats. If you're allergic to dairy, use a plain plant milk you tolerate; changing the milk doesn't make oats safe for an oat allergy.
Recipe of the day
Green Tea Refresh Toner
TimeLet dry naturally, about 1 to 2 minutes
How often1 to 2 times a day, as tolerated
Shelf lifeMake fresh, use once cooled, and discard leftovers
Ingredients
  • 1 tsp plain loose-leaf green tea or 1 tea bag
  • 1/2 cup hot water, about 176°F
Method
  1. 1Steep the tea for 5 minutes in water at about 176°F.
  2. 2Strain into a clean container and let it cool completely.
  3. 3If you'd like it cooler, briefly chill it in a covered container in the refrigerator, then use during the same session.

How to use: Gently sweep a fresh cotton round dampened with the cooled tea over clean skin, or pat on with clean hands. Avoid your eyes and follow with moisturizer. In the morning, finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen.

Heads up: Patch-test at least 24 hours before first use. Keep the tea light; a strong brew may leave skin feeling dry. Skip flavored teas and stop if your skin feels tight or irritated.
Busy day? Gently cleanse, use your usual moisturizer, apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen, and check off the day. Tonight, gentle cleansing and moisturizer are enough. The Oat Cloud Mask is an optional extra if you've already tested it.
Good to know: Oily or acne-prone skin: skip the Rich Comfort Cream. Use your usual lightweight moisturizer; an already-tested thin layer of Aloe Comfort Gel is optional.

Fresh-Faced Glow

Recipe of the day
Smooth-Feel Flaxseed Mask
TimeUp to 15 minutes; rinse sooner if it feels tight
How often1 to 2 times a week, as tolerated
Shelf lifeUse once fully cooled and discard leftovers
Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp whole flaxseed
  • 1 cup distilled water
Method
  1. 1In a clean saucepan, gently simmer the flaxseed and water over low heat, stirring for 8 to 10 minutes until a translucent gel forms.
  2. 2Carefully strain while still warm through a clean fine-mesh strainer. Let it cool enough to handle safely, and don't squeeze hot gel with your hands.
  3. 3Let the strained gel cool completely before applying.

How to use: With clean fingers, apply a thin layer to clean face and neck skin, avoiding your eyes and lips. Rinse gently with lukewarm water before the film feels tight or uncomfortable.

Heads up: Patch-test at least 24 hours before first use. Any smoother-looking effect is temporary and may fade with rinsing. Tightness isn't proof of a benefit. Homemade flaxseed gel has no tested preservation system; don't save it for later.
Recipe of the day
DIY Hydrating Sheet Mask
Time10 to 15 minutes, or less if the cotton starts to dry
How often2 times a week, as tolerated
Shelf lifeUse immediately; discard the cotton and leftover liquid
Ingredients
  • 1 large cotton round or 2 cotton rounds gently split into thinner layers
  • 3 tbsp freshly made Rice Water Glow
  • 1 tbsp freshly made, cooled Green Tea Refresh Toner
Method
  1. 1With clean hands, gently separate the cotton rounds into soft, thin layers. Use them as small patches rather than stretching them into a full-face sheet.
  2. 2Mix the freshly made rice water and cooled green tea in a clean shallow bowl.
  3. 3Soak the cotton, then gently squeeze out enough liquid to prevent dripping.

How to use: Place the damp cotton on clean cheeks or forehead, keeping your eyes, nose, and mouth clear. Relax, and take it off as soon as it starts to feel dry. Gently rinse your skin and follow with moisturizer.

Heads up: Patch-test the combined liquid at least 24 hours before first use. Don't sleep in the mask, reuse the cotton, or let it dry completely on your skin. Keep all homemade mixtures away from your eyes.

Honey & Yogurt Comfort Mask
Time15 minutes
How often1 to 2 times a week, as tolerated
Shelf lifeUse immediately and discard leftovers
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp plain honey

Recipe of the day
Neck & Chest Comfort Cream
TimeAllow about 5 minutes to settle; a light coating may remain
How oftenEvery evening, as tolerated
Shelf lifeShelf life hasn't been tested; make a small batch and keep cool, clean, and completely dry
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp plain, fragrance-free shea butter
  • 1 tsp pure jojoba oil
  • 3 drops pure rosehip seed oil, optional
Method
  1. 1Gently melt the shea butter in a clean, dry heatproof bowl over warm water, keeping water out.
  2. 2Remove from the heat and stir in the oils. Chill in the refrigerator for 15 minutes, then beat with a clean, dry fork until creamy. Let cool completely before applying.

How to use: With clean hands, gently smooth a small amount over your upper chest and neck after moisturizer. Use light strokes without pulling the skin or pressing on the front of your neck.

Heads up: Patch-test at least 24 hours before first use. Use plain rosehip seed carrier oil, not rose essential oil. Scoop with a clean, dry spatula and discard if contaminated or changed. My Skin Feels Tight, Burns, or Looks Red

Rinse gently with cool to lukewarm water and stop using the mixture. Pause the Challenge and avoid applying homemade products to irritated skin. Contact a dermatologist if symptoms persist or you're unsure. Get urgent medical help for trouble breathing or swelling of the lips or tongue.

Oily Skin

Skip the Rich Comfort Cream and use your usual lightweight moisturizer. Keep the Honey & Yogurt Comfort Mask and Creamy Yogurt Mask on for no more than 10 minutes, as tolerated. If you use oil, try just 1 or 2 drops on dry areas.
